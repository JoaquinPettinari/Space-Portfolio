## Prompt

Create a production-quality 360 degree equirectangular environment texture for a cinematic spacecraft observatory in Blender. Exactly 2:1 aspect ratio panoramic spherical map, with continuous matching left/right edges and quiet dark pole regions. This is a distant SPACE BACKGROUND ONLY: absolutely no planets, no moons, no asteroids, no ships, no foreground objects, no text. Photorealistic astronomical deep-space art, not a stylized illustration. Across the central middle latitudes, an enormous oblique spiral galaxy runs diagonally from lower left to upper right with its luminous ivory-peach nucleus centered at 62% width 45% height. Its arms are asymmetric, filled with exquisitely fine branching dark molecular dust lanes, intricate wispy cobalt and indigo nebula filaments and tiny sparse magenta/copper emission clusters. Bright inner core is diffuse irregular light interrupted by complex dark dust, never concentric rings, no simple geometric swirls or perfect curves. Resolve abundant microscopic stellar granularity, with tiny sharp varied-size stars embedded in the arms, not large equal dots. Deep velvety black negative space on both outer sides, allowing existing 3D planets in Blender to read clearly. A much smaller distant galaxy on the far-left region and delicate faint nebula structures on the opposite hemisphere give interest across the full 360 degrees without crowding it. Strong realistic high dynamic-range appearance, luminous restrained highlights, very subtle scattering, richly detailed but not oversaturated; exquisite cinematic astrophotography aesthetic akin a high-end science-fiction film space matte painting. No lens flares, no border, no horizon, no Earth. Output highest detail panorama suitable as a world texture.
