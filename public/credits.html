Visual assets

Asterion: fictional planets, rings and orbital fragments authored in Blender for CRUZ DEL SUR. The galactic background combines procedural stars and nebulae with AI-generated galaxy artwork. This is a cinematic environment, not an astronomical simulation.

Previous exterior assets retained in the project: Earth day, night and cloud maps, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Moon and Milky Way textures by Solar System Scope, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Adaptations: resized, compressed to WebP and rendered with custom lighting and atmospheric shading. The planetary arrangement and sizes are cinematic, not an astronomical simulation. Saturn's rings are procedural.
